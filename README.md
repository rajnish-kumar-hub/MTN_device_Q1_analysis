<h1> MTN Device Performance - Q1 Overview</h1>


<h2>Revenue Insights</h2>
<ul>
  <li><p> <b>5G Broadband Router</b> brings in the <b>highest revenue</b> each month - much higher         than other devices.</p></li>
  <li><p> <b>Mobile SIM Card shows the lowest revenue, though it has the highest customer count</p>      </li>
</ul>


<h2>Customer Count</h2>
<ul>
  <li><p> <b>Mobile SIM Card</b> has the <b>most customers</b> (301), but revenue is low - indicating lower ARPU(Average Revenue Per User).</p></li>
  <li><p><b>4G Router</b> has the <b>smallest customer base</b>(216)</p></li>
</ul>


<h2>Churn Insights</h2>
<ul>
  <li><p> <b>Mobile SIM Card</b> has the <b>highest male data usage</b> (~108.5 units).</p></li>
  <li><p><b>5G Broadband Router and Mobile SIM Card</b> show relatively high<b> female data usage</b>(~98.8 and 107.4 units)</p></li>
</ul>

<h2> Average Tenure </h2>
<ul>
<li><p><b>5G Broadband Router customers have the <b>longest average tenure</b>(~32.2-32.4 months) - a sign of loyalty. </p> </li>
  
<li><p><b>4G Router</b> and <b>Moible SIM Card</b> customers have shorter tenure (~30.5-31.6 months).</p> </li>
</ul>

<h2>Customer Demographics</h2>
<ul>
  <li>
    <p>
      <b>Customer age </b> is consistently around 47-49 years across devices - skewed slightly             older.
    </p>
  </li>
  <li>
    <p>
      <b> Gender split </b> is quite balanced across devices, but <b>Mobile SIM Card</b> has               slightly more female users(154 vs 147).
    </p>
  </li>
</ul>

<h2>Customer Satisfaction</h2>
<ul>
  <li>
    <p>
      <b>Broadband WiFi and 5G Broadband Router</b> show the <b> highest satisfaction scores</b> (just above 3.0).
    </p>
  </li>
  <li>
    <p>
      <b>Mobile SIM Card</b> and <b>4G Router</b> show the lowest satisfaction (~2.8-2.9), matching their high churn and low revenue patterns.
    </p>
  </li>
  
</ul>

<h2>Overall Insights</h2>
<ul>
  <li>
    <p>
      <b>5G Broadband Router</b> is your <b>star performer:</b> highest revenue, longest tenure, high satisfaction, and low churn.
    </p>
  </li>
  <li>
    <p>
      <b> Mobile SIM Card </b> needs attention: despite large customer base, it struggles with             churn, low revenue, and low satisfaction.
    </p>
    </li>
    <li>
     <p> There's an opportunity to <b> upsell MiFi or Broadband</b> devices to high-churn SIM             customers.</p>
    </p>
  </li>
</ul>
